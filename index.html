About me

I am an AI leader and researcher, born in Belém do Pará, Brazil, and living in Japan since 2016. I hold a Ph.D. in Systems Engineering from the University of Tsukuba, and I now lead the AI division at Nohara Group — a construction group with over 300 years of history — running 9+ concurrent projects across Japan, Singapore, and Indonesia.

My work sits across three pillars that keep feeding each other: research, engineering, and management. I started as a researcher, publishing on evolutionary computation and nonlinear optimization and securing a $200,000 CNPq grant. I became an engineer who ships production systems — deep-learning models at 95% accuracy, autonomous detection pipelines, weather forecasting inside aviation maps. Today I combine machine learning, optimization, and agentic AI with Lean practice to deliver measurable business results.

I also write Nohara’s AI governance frameworks, run reskilling and AI literacy programmes, and coach data-science teams across three countries.
